# Over the Gear

![Game Screenshot](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMzc2NzM0OC8yMjQ3MzUwNi5wbmc=/347x500/U9J%2Flb.png) 

**Over the Gear** is a cute game about jumping gear teeth. 

You're a guy named **Cog**, and you fell into a clock or a factory, somehow (I'm as clueless about the story as you are), and now you have to avoid your impending doom by not touching the gear's teeth. 

As you progress, you meet cool friends as Cog traverses wherever he is. This will not be explained in-game in any way, shape, or form.

---

## ✨ Features
* **Physical Activity:** Get some digital exercise.
* **Vibrant Visuals:** Look at cool colors while you struggle to survive.
* **High Stakes:** Die and die some more.
* **"Features":** The coin thingy has a **15% chance** to have knockback. This is definitely a feature and not a bug. Why would a game have bugs anyways?

## 🎮 How to Play
It’s simple, really. If anyone dislikes jumping, this game will save your life.

* **Jump:** `Spacebar`
* **Goal:** Don't touch the teeth. Collect the "coin thingies."
